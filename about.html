About Me

Hi! I'm the lead designer at Creative Studio. With over a decade of product design experience at top tech companies, I've built everything from complex enterprise dashboards to award-winning consumer mobile apps.

I am incredibly passionate about teaching practical, real-world design skills that get you hired. My courses focus heavily on the 'why' behind design decisions, ensuring you don't just learn the tools, but also develop a strong product mindset. When I'm not designing or teaching, you can find me hiking or experimenting with new coffee brewing methods.
